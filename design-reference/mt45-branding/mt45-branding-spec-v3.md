## Current layout

- White truck body remains exposed; this is a cost-effective spot-vinyl package, not a wrap.
- Side: official navy-and-gold vehicle lockup on the upper cargo panel, no QR code.
- Rear: official navy-and-gold vehicle lockup above a white-backed QR action panel.
- Secondary copy: `(000) 000-0000` and `sheltonuniformandlinen.com`.
- The service descriptor inside the official lockup remains `COMMERCIAL LAUNDRY · SAN DIEGO` until a new vehicle tagline is approved and issued as a revised brand asset.

## QR verification and production caution

- The QR in the completed rear mockup was decoded from the full mockup image and returned `https://sheltonuniformandlinen.com`.
- The rear roll-up door has moving horizontal seams. A fabricator should either use a rigid seam-safe panel or scan-test the installed code after every seam is cut.
- Final decal dimensions must be based on physical truck measurements, not inferred from the photographs.
