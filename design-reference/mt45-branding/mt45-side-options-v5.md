# Shelton MT45 Side Options — V5

All three concepts are side-only, omit the QR code, use the approved Shelton palette, and retain the canonical circled-S path. The name/divider/descriptor artwork is taken directly from the canonical short-divider vehicle SVG and rendered with the approved project fonts.

## Option 1 — Enlarged wording

File: `shelton-mt45-side-option-1-enlarged-wording-v5.png`

- Direct revision of the user-supplied pass.
- Circled-S retains the same approximate visual size and position.
- Company name, descriptor, phone, and website are enlarged for road readability.
- Lowest material and installation cost.

## Option 2 — Route stripe

File: `shelton-mt45-side-option-2-route-stripe-v5.png`

- Uses the truck's long body and existing rail as a directional device.
- Gold cap line and navy contact stripe increase distance legibility.
- Small canonical mark on the cab door strengthens fleet recognition.
- Moderate material use with straightforward installation.

## Option 3 — Folded motion

File: `shelton-mt45-side-option-3-folded-motion-v5.png`

- Navy-and-gold rear-quarter slashes suggest folded linens and forward route motion.
- Keeps most of the white truck exposed.
- Strongest personality with simple two-color geometry.
- Slightly more alignment and trimming labor than Option 2.
