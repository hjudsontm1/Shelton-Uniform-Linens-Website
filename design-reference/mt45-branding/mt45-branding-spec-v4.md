## Side direction

- Deep-navy partial-vinyl field limited to the uninterrupted upper cargo panel.
- One muted-gold diagonal edge suggests a folded linen and gives the layout forward motion.
- Canonical `shelton-vehicle-lockup-dark.svg` is placed unchanged on the navy field; this is the approved short-divider vehicle lockup.
- Secondary cream contact line: `(000) 000-0000 · sheltonuniformandlinen.com`.
- Canonical navy circled-S mark on the cab door.
- Cab, lower cargo body, wheels, and rear quarter remain white; this is not a full wrap.

## Rear direction

- Canonical `shelton-lockup-stacked-light.svg` restores the preferred hierarchy: circled S above the company name.
- QR at left and the existing action copy at right remain in the liked lower-door composition.
- The QR is rendered on a 41-module, eight-pixels-per-module grid with its complete quiet zone.
- The completed full rear mockup scan-tests to `https://sheltonuniformandlinen.com`.

## Copy note

The last line currently remains the website, `sheltonuniformandlinen.com`. No approved email address was found in the project, so an email address was not invented.

## Production caution

The rear roll-up door has moving horizontal seams. Before fabrication, the installer should propose either a rigid seam-safe QR panel or an installation method that can be scan-tested with the door fully closed. Final dimensions must come from physical truck measurements.
