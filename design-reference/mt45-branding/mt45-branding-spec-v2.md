# Shelton MT45 Branding Direction — V2

## Recommended production approach

- Preserve the existing white body; no full or partial wrap.
- Use deep-navy (`#081321`) high-performance cut vinyl for the mark and lettering.
- Keep the muted-gold (`#B8965A`) rule optional. Removing it makes the primary package one-color.
- Use one laminated, white-backed printed decal for the QR action panel.
- Leave the cab doors and all lower body panels unbranded.

## Passenger / curb side

- Keep every graphic on the uninterrupted upper cargo panel above the rub rail.
- Lead with the circled-S, followed by the Shelton name and service descriptor.
- Keep the service line, phone, and plain-text URL within the main lockup.
- Put the QR action panel at the far rear of the same flat panel.
- Recommended installed QR artwork size: approximately 18 inches square, subject to an onsite panel measurement.
- Use the passenger side as the primary scan location because the substrate is flat and the truck is commonly viewed while parked at a stop.

## Driver side

- Repeat the main Shelton lockup and contact information.
- Omit the second QR for the lowest-cost package, or repeat it only if route use shows a clear need.

## Rear

- Use the centered Shelton mark, name, phone, and plain-text URL.
- Do not treat the rear QR mockup as production-ready without installer approval: the roll-up door has moving horizontal seams that would cut through the code.
- If a rear QR is requested, the installer must provide a seam-safe method and scan-test the closed door after every seam has been cut.

## QR production requirements

- Encoded destination: `https://sheltonuniformandlinen.com`
- Error correction: H
- Geometry: standard square modules and square finder patterns
- Color: deep navy on solid white
- Preserve the complete four-module quiet zone on all sides.
- Do not add a center logo, caption inside the code, rounded modules, transparency, or a patterned background.
- Use the supplied SVG for production and the PNG for previews.
- Scan-test the printed proof, the installed decal, and the finished truck at several useful distances and angles.

## Current placeholder content

- Phone: `(000) 000-0000`
- Website: `sheltonuniformandlinen.com`
- Service line: `COMMERCIAL LINEN • UNIFORMS • ROUTE SERVICE`

Final fabrication dimensions must be based on physical measurements of the truck, not measurements inferred from the photographs.
