# Shelton Project Brand Source

The canonical, approved commercial identity for this project lives in:

`assets/Shelton Brand Assets/brand/`

Before creating or editing any Shelton-branded visual, read that folder's `README.md` and use the supplied source assets directly.

## Required brand rules

- Do not redraw, approximate, regenerate, stretch, skew, or restyle the Shelton circled-S mark.
- Do not substitute AI-rendered logo lettering for an approved lockup.
- For vehicles, route equipment, and signage, start with `shelton-vehicle-lockup-light.svg` or `shelton-vehicle-lockup-dark.svg`.
- For one-color light vehicle production, use the navy mark/lockup variants where appropriate.
- Preserve the approved palette, proportions, clear space, and typography rules documented in the brand README.
- The Shelton Cleaners wordmark and 24/7 kiosk badge are reference-only and are not the Shelton Linen & Uniform Services commercial identity.
- Convert editable SVG text to outlines, or package the approved fonts, before vendor production handoff.

Treat the assets in this folder as the source of truth for website, vehicle, print, embroidery, signage, presentation, and social applications.
