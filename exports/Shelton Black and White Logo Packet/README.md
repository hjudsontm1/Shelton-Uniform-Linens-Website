# Shelton Black and White Logo Packet

Pure one-color versions of the selected Shelton Linen & Uniform Services identity.

## Layouts

1. **Primary Horizontal** — Vehicles, signage, website headers.
2. **Signature Horizontal** — Wide print, storefronts, presentations.
3. **Panoramic All In One** — The complete identity on one uninterrupted line.
4. **Stacked Centered** — Posters, square layouts, apparel backs.
5. **Compact Vertical** — Narrow panels, garment fronts, banners.
6. **Wordmark Only** — Minimal headers and supporting applications.
7. **Editorial Split** — Statement signage and premium collateral.

Each layout is provided as:

- Solid black transparent outlined SVG
- Solid white transparent outlined SVG
- High-resolution transparent black PNG
- High-resolution transparent white PNG

The system retains the selected Inter ampersand 04, native-stem capital I 02, enlarged commercial descriptor, revised compact vertical spacing, and the unchanged canonical circled-S.

Use black artwork on white or light materials. Use white artwork reversed from black, dark, photographic, or colored materials. These files are especially suited to embroidery, vinyl cutting, screen printing, engraving, stamps, and single-ink production.

Open `Shelton-Black-White-Gallery.html` for easy local review and downloads.

The supplied Sharp Grotesk font is a personal-use trial. License Sharp Grotesk before commercial production or public deployment.
